The longer view

The yield curve

One observation date per curve. Missing tenors are omitted; no 25-year yield is estimated. Government benchmarks differ across countries.

The country ledger

Rates and yields change in basis points (bp). Inflation and GDP growth change in percentage points (pp), versus the previous reference period. Currency changes are percentages. Each market comparison ends on that series’ latest observation date.

Sources, freshness and coverage

Data is checked daily after deployment. This is not an intraday price feed. Weekends and holidays can leave the most recent observation unchanged. Observation dates and download times are distinct; publication timestamps are not consistently supplied by the sources.

Singapore policy is based on the exchange rate; SORA is an overnight market rate, not a policy target. This version connects Singapore FX through a derived BNM cross-rate. Singapore SORA, CPI, GDP and SGS yields, and Malaysian MGS yields and MYOR, currently require a verified CSV import. No unavailable figure is filled with a guess.

Comparisons use the latest observation on or before the lookback date, showing the actual dates. Monthly and quarterly indicators compare reference periods. Initial BNM history accumulates with daily collections. A successful download does not mean a new observation was published.
